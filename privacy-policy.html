Privacy Policy

How BARINV PRO handles information in the Admin app, Barback Link, Barback Manager, and related operational services.

On this page Scope Information Device access How it is used Sharing Storage and retention Choices and requests Security Contact

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Operational app policy. This policy is separate from the privacy policy for the public BARINV.ca marketing website. A venue or organization using BARINV may also have its own privacy obligations and policies.

1. Scope

This policy applies to BARINV PRO operational software and services, including the iPhone and iPad Admin app, the web Admin interface, Barback Link, Barback Manager, and related authentication, database, synchronization, and support services.

BARINV is designed for authorized venue operators and staff. The venue or organization that provides access may control the operational records entered by its users. Questions about a venue's staffing or operational records should normally be directed to that venue first.

3. Camera, Bluetooth, network, and local storage

Camera and barcode scanning

When an authorized user starts the scanner, BARINV uses camera access to detect item barcodes. Camera access is not required for manual item lookup. BARINV is not designed to retain photographs merely because the barcode scanner was used.

Bluetooth and scale readings

When an authorized user chooses to connect a supported scale, BARINV uses Bluetooth to receive local device readings. Users can instead enter grams manually. Scale readings and derived bottle percentages are operational estimates and should be checked by the operator.

Network access

BARINV uses network access to authenticate, retrieve authorized venue data, synchronize Events and inventory workflows, call narrowly scoped server functions, and load approved web content.

On-device storage

BARINV may store session state, preferences, drafts, offline/outbox submissions, item data needed for the current workflow, and local scale profile or calibration values on the device or in browser storage. Authorized users should use managed devices and sign out when access is no longer required.

4. How information is used

  • Provide BARINV features requested by an authorized venue.
  • Authenticate users and enforce Venue, Night, role, staff, and Bar permissions.
  • Record, review, synchronize, and audit inventory and operational Events.
  • Maintain security, prevent duplicate submissions, and investigate misuse or failures.
  • Provide support and communicate about service operation.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce applicable agreements.

BARINV is not designed to sell personal information or use venue operational data for third-party advertising.

5. When information is shared

Operational information is available to authorized users of the relevant venue according to their role and scope. BARINV may also use service providers for authentication, database hosting, server functions, web hosting, content delivery, email, security, and support. Supabase provides core authentication and hosted backend services for the current BARINV platform.

Information may also be processed by integrations enabled by the venue, disclosed when required by law, or used to protect users, BARINV, or others from security threats or abuse. BARINV does not authorize service providers to use venue operational data for their own advertising.

6. Storage, location, and retention

BARINV and its service providers may process information in Canada, the United States, or other locations where approved providers operate. Privacy protections may differ between jurisdictions.

Retention depends on the type of record, the venue's configuration and agreement, operational audit requirements, security needs, and applicable law. Synced Events and audit records may be retained as business records. Local drafts and outbox entries remain on the device until synchronized, removed through an authorized workflow, or cleared with the related app/browser data. Support correspondence is retained as reasonably needed to resolve the request and maintain business records.

7. Access, correction, deletion, and choices

Users can decline camera or Bluetooth permission and use available manual workflows. Device permissions can be changed in system or browser settings, although some optional features will then be unavailable.

For access, correction, deletion, or account questions, contact the venue administrator that issued access. Privacy requests concerning BARINV's own handling can also be sent to info@barinv.ca. BARINV may need to verify identity, authority, and the applicable venue before acting. Some records may need to be retained for security, audit, contractual, or legal reasons.

8. Security and user responsibilities

BARINV uses access controls, scoped sessions, audit records, transport security, and other safeguards intended to protect operational data. No system can guarantee absolute security. Users must protect account credentials and PINs, avoid sharing access links, use approved devices, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access.

BARINV is intended for business users and is not directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as BARINV features, providers, or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Material changes may also be communicated through the app, website, venue administrator, or other appropriate channel.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to info@barinv.ca. Include the venue name and the account email or staff identifier relevant to the request, but do not send passwords, PINs, access links, database keys, or full inventory exports.
