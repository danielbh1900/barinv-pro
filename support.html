Never send secrets. Support will not ask for your password, staff PIN, access link, API key, database credential, or complete authentication token. Do not include those values in email, screenshots, CSV files, or attachments.

Contact BARINV support

Email info@barinv.ca for BARINV PRO app and web support.

Email Support Privacy Policy

Response times depend on issue severity, current support capacity, and any separate customer agreement. This page does not create a guaranteed support response time.

What to include

  • Venue name and Night/date.
  • Your role and the affected BARINV surface: Admin app, web Admin, Barback Link, or Barback Manager.
  • Device model, operating system, browser, and BARINV app version when visible.
  • The action you attempted and the exact on-screen error without credentials.
  • Whether the issue affects one Event, one staff member, one Bar, or the whole venue.
  • A redacted screenshot when useful. Remove customer, credential, and unrelated staff information.

Event-night safety

BARINV is not an emergency service. If an issue could cause unsafe service, unauthorized access, or incorrect inventory movement, stop the affected workflow and notify the on-duty manager. Preserve visible drafts, outbox warnings, and error details. Do not repeatedly resubmit an operation when its result is uncertain.

Do not approve, reject, delete, or recreate business Events solely to troubleshoot unless the venue manager has confirmed the operational impact.

Login, PIN, and assigned access

For expired links, revoked sessions, missing Bars, staff assignments, role changes, or a forgotten PIN, contact the venue manager that issued access. Support may require the venue's authorized administrator to confirm an access change.

Do not share an active Barback link or PIN in a support email. A manager should revoke exposed access and create a new session through the approved workflow.

Draft, outbox, and synchronization issues

If BARINV reports that device storage is full or an outbox save failed, do not close or reload the page. Keep the page open, capture the visible queue count and warning, and contact the on-duty manager or support. When connectivity returns, use the normal retry or Submit All workflow and verify the final status.

If a submission result is uncertain, use the Events Log and its current Venue/Night filters to check for the Event before submitting it again.

Camera scanner and Bluetooth scale

Camera and Bluetooth access are optional. If permission is denied or a device is unsupported, use item search and manual grams when available. Confirm the selected item, stable weight, calibration, bottle state, and quantity before adding an Event for review.

Remote Scale Bridge readings are not accepted as trusted inventory weight in the public-release safety configuration. Do not rely on a remote broadcast to populate inventory measurements.

Privacy and account requests

For access, correction, deletion, or account questions, contact the venue administrator first. Requests about BARINV's own handling of personal information can be sent to info@barinv.ca.

Include the venue name and relevant account email or staff identifier. BARINV may need to verify identity and authority. Do not send passwords, PINs, access links, database keys, or full inventory exports.

Legal information

Use of BARINV PRO is governed by the App Terms of Use. Information handling is described in the App Privacy Policy. Separate signed venue agreements control where they conflict with these public pages.
